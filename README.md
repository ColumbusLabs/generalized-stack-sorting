# Generalized stack permutations

Papers, exact algebraic certificates, and reproducible Lean sources from Columbus Labs.

A generalized `(r,s)`-stack inserts each incoming entry into one of its top `r` positions and removes an entry from one of its top `s` positions. The stack has unrestricted height. This repository studies the distinct permutations generated by that machine and, equivalently by inversion, the permutations it can sort.

## Papers

- [Algebraic enumeration of generalized stack permutations](papers/algebraicity/generalized-stack-algebraicity.pdf). Algebraicity for every fixed pair of positive widths, with a degree-12 equation for `(3,3)`. [TeX](papers/algebraicity/generalized-stack-algebraicity.tex) · [Reading copy](papers/algebraicity/generalized-stack-algebraicity.md)
- [A direct enumeration of permutations sortable by a generalized (2,2)-stack](papers/enumeration/main.pdf). A mechanism-based derivation of the known cubic. [TeX](papers/enumeration/main.tex) · [Reading copy](papers/enumeration/main.md)
- [Physical capacity in a generalized stack: exact series and a Brownian limit](papers/capacity/capacity.pdf). Exact capacity series, minimum-memory statistics, and asymptotic capacity. [TeX](papers/capacity/capacity.tex) · [Reading copy](papers/capacity/capacity.md)
- [Finite and infinite bases for generalized stacks](papers/classification/classification.pdf). Constructive obstruction families and the capacity threshold. [TeX](papers/classification/classification.tex) · [Reading copy](papers/classification/classification.md)

## What is formalized

The `(3,3)` endpoint identifies the all-length count of distinct outputs of the literal unrestricted-height machine with its scalar branch and proves the polynomial identity. Its complete source closure contains 1,613 Lean modules. The all-width algebraicity argument and degree-minimality argument are written proofs; the `(3,3)` Lean endpoint does not formalize them.

The companion certificates cover the `(2,2)` enumeration, the exact bounded-capacity recurrence, and the stated generalized-stack basis and container results. Their limits are described in [formal scope](formal/README.md). Finite checks corroborate implementations; they are not substituted for all-length proofs.

## Reproduce

Start with [REPRODUCE.md](REPRODUCE.md). It separates source-integrity checks, bounded Python tests, exact-algebra verification, PDF builds, and source-only Lean compilation. No compiler binaries or compiled project objects are distributed.

- `papers/`: manuscripts, bibliography, and displayed equation data
- `src/` and `tests/`: executable finite models and regression tests
- `docs/` and `research/`: supporting mathematical arguments and exact algebraic data
- `formal/`: Lean sources, pinned toolchains, dependency manifests, and replay scripts
- `MANIFEST.json`: exact release-file hashes
