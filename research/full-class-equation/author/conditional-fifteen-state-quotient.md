# Conditional 15-state residual quotient and graph certificate

Derivation, 10 October 2026. All implications in this note are exact linear algebra. Its matrix-shape hypotheses hold in retained branch jets but have not yet been proved for the selected formal branch. This note is a route to an all-word certificate, not that certificate itself.

Use the notation of `compressed-residual-automaton.md`. Assume:

1. B has nonzero columns only at I=(0,1,4), with rows B_0=e1, B_1=e4; its submatrix C=B[I,I] is invertible. Its constant determinant in the retained candidate is 12.
2. a=alpha_z=e0+p(e1-e3)+q(e4-e5), and Z=B[:,I] R, where R has rows a,e3,e5.

Then rank B=rank Z=3 and their left kernels agree. Indeed R has rank three, since its columns 0,3,5 form a unit triangular matrix. Moreover L=B-Z has row space span(e1-e3,e4-e5), with rows L_0=e1-e3 and L_1=e4-e5, and hence rank two.

## Explicit quotient map

Let V=B[:,I] C^(-1), W=L[:,(1,4)], and mu=beta-V beta_I. Then V_I=I_3, W_(0,1)=I_2, and mu_I=0.

Map an ordinary suffix-automaton row (f,j,k,e), of block sizes (6,6,6,1), to

    (f V, j V, k W, e+(f+j) mu),

of block sizes (3,3,2,1). Denote this 19-by-9 matrix by S. Let J be the 9-by-19 section selecting the F and J rows with indices I, K rows with indices 0,1, and the E row. Then J S=I_9.

The kernel of S has dimension ten. It consists of F/J rows in the common left kernel of B and Z, with their empty-word values compensated in the E coordinate, and K rows in the left kernel of L. These rows annihilate every suffix output. Their invariance follows directly from the displayed N_z,N_b,N_t matrices: F/J common-kernel rows die after any nonempty input, while K rows in ker L also die after any nonempty input.

Therefore

    N'_s=J N_s S,
    N_s S=S N'_s,
    nu=S nu',
    nu'=(beta_I,beta_I,0,1)^tr.

The full quotient residual automaton has dimension fifteen:

    Q'_s=[[M_s,-C_s S],[0,N'_s]],
    tau'=(kappa,nu')^tr.

Project each of the six initial residual rows by diag(I_6,S). The two empty residual scalars are unchanged. Since the quotient intertwines and preserves the terminal column, this transformation preserves every residual word value exactly.

## Six-row graph certificate

A particularly concrete all-word certificate now consists of a 6-by-9 matrix H such that

    M_s H = H N'_s-C_s S   for s=z,b,t,
    kappa+H nu'=0,

and each projected initial row has the form (v,vH). Include the two empty residual scalar equations. Then the six-dimensional row space generated by [I_6,H] is invariant under every Q'_s and annihilates tau', proving all-word compatibility.

The graph H is forced by the six B-family residual rows associated with words B,T,Z,BB,BZ,BBB, if they lie in such a graph. Their prefix projections are e1,e2,e3,e4,e5,e4 B. The resulting 6-by-6 prefix matrix has determinant equal, up to sign, to B_(4,0), whose constant term is 12. Thus H can be expressed as a rational function of the original unknown matrices and boundary data using a unit denominator; no x-adic division is needed.

The remaining obligations are to prove the shape hypotheses and the finite graph-certificate identities on the selected formal branch, or independently construct a formal solution satisfying all of them. A finite truncation satisfying these identities does not settle either obligation.
