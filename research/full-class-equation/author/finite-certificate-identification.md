# Identification of any regular finite-certificate solution with full G(3,3)

Exact conditional theorem, 10 October 2026. The premise is existence of formal power-series parameters satisfying the complete finite graph and scalar certificate. This note proves the counting identification once that premise is met; it does not prove the premise.

Let the eight parameters be formal power series over a characteristic-zero field, with constant tuple (12,8,-4/9,1/18,9/2,2,6,9). Construct B,T,Z,alpha and the graph blocks using the frozen rational formulas. Every denominator used there is a unit at this tuple. Let beta,r be six-component formal columns and d0 a formal scalar, satisfying:

1. r=beta+xT r.
2. Every E-column transition and initial-row containment condition of the 15-state graph certificate, with H_E=U beta.
3. The terminal condition beta-xr+H nu'=0.
4. e0(beta-xr)=2d0 and alpha(beta-xr)=2d0.
5. d0=1+2x d0+x^2 r0.

Here H=[H_F,H_J,H_K,H_E] and nu'=(beta_I,beta_I,0,1)^tr. These are finitely many rational equations; denominators can be cleared using the recorded unit conditions. The eight-parameter identities supply all non-E transition and initial-row conditions automatically.

## All-word implication

The row graph generated by [I_6,H] is invariant under the three 15-state residual transitions and annihilates the terminal column. Each of the six initial residual rows belongs to it. Therefore every nonempty-word residual vanishes. Assumption4 handles the two empty-word residuals. The explicit 19-to9 quotient intertwining preserves each residual value, so the original exact signed-word recurrence is satisfied for every valid state word.

Define a completion series on all valid three-letter states by

    Phi(empty)=g0=1+x d0,
    Phi(Z)=d0,
    Phi(ZB w)=e0 M_w beta,
    Phi(ZZ w)=alpha M_w beta.

Extend this linearly to signed word polynomials. The two boundary recurrences proven by residual vanishing, assumptions1 and5, and g0=1+x d0 supply the exact root, height-one, record and held-deletion recurrence. The count is unrestricted: every possible record jump and every held-output rank occurs; no capacity or rank cutoff is imposed.

## Coefficientwise uniqueness

For a state S of held height h and n future input tokens, every recurrence summand has total n+h reduced by exactly one. A held deletion lowers h by one. A record using j+1 new inputs leaves h+j held labels and n-j-1 future labels. Only the empty state with n=0 has the empty-output contribution. Thus induction on n+h proves uniqueness of every coefficient, even though the state-indexed recurrence is infinite.

The normal form counts distinct output permutations by unique lazy codes. The O-run relabelling and clause valuation preserve its unrestricted completion counts. Consequently the unique coefficients of the signed recurrence are precisely those actual counts. The finite-certificate solution therefore satisfies

    g0=G(3,3),
    G(3,3)=1+x+x^2 beta0.

If normalized by t=beta2/beta0 and beta0=2/(1-xt), this becomes

    G(3,3)=1+x+2x^2/(1-xt).

This argument supplies counting uniqueness independently of uniqueness of the scalar parameter branch. To announce a finite system for G, existence of at least one regular formal solution of the complete finite certificate must still be certified. A solution of only a selected subset of its equations is not sufficient.
